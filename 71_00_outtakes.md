# Outtakes

Across the production cycle, I discarded far more tracks in various stages of development than are documented here. This section is neither an exhaustive catalog nor a "secret vault." Rather, I selected two specific pieces that provide essential context for understanding the album's conceptual evolution:

* **[Excellence](./71_01_excellence.md):** Documents an early thematic trajectory that was subsequently abandoned.
* **[Masks](./71_02_masks.md):** A failed conceptual draft that nevertheless directly inspired several album tracks, most notably *[Jobs](./70_04_jobs.md)*.
