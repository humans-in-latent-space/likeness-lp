# Contributing & Open Intent Engagement

Thank you for your interest in *Likeness*.


## 1. A Finite Monograph, Not a Rolling Codebase

*Likeness* is a finished artistic work and a methodological monograph rather than an open-source software library or an ongoing collaborative product. The musical work itself has been fully mixed, mastered, and delivered to digital streaming platforms (DSPs) via DistroKid under the independent imprint **[Humans in Latent Space](https://github.com/humans-in-latent-space)**.

Because the released audio assets are cryptographically and distributionally locked to the DSP release, modifications to audio stems, prompt histories, mix parameters, or track arrangements are impossible without triggering a disruptive, full-scale DSP reissue. Similarly, the project records, logs, and essays represent an archival trace of a concluded production cycle.


## 2. Pull Requests and Merge Policy

For the reasons outlined above, **direct Pull Requests (PRs) or Merge Requests will not be accepted or merged into this repository.**

- **Documentation Errata:** If you spot broken links, factual discrepancies, or typographical errors in the documentation, please feel free to open a GitHub Issue.
- **Creative & Technical Trajectory:** PRs proposing alternative stems, modified prompt compositions, automated audio filters, or divergent compositional branches will be politely closed, as they conflict with the singular, closed nature of this creative record.


## 3. Forking, Adaptation, and Apache 2.0

While direct contribution to the authoritative upstream repository is closed, **downstream exploration and divergent evolution are unconditionally encouraged.**

All documentation, prompt architectures, configuration scripts, and project assets in this repository are licensed under the **Apache License 2.0**. Under this license, you are explicitly invited to:

- **Fork** the repository and repurpose the prompts, production workflows, and evaluation rubrics for your own creative projects.
- **Analyze** the instructions, lyrics, and metadata as empirical data for research in generative music, agentic orchestration, or digital humanities.
- **Publish** derivative methodologies, critical commentary, or independent artistic forks (subject to the attribution terms of Apache 2.0).


## 4. Critical Feedback and Discourse

Feedback, scholarly inquiry, and philosophical debate are warmly welcomed:

- **Issues & Discussions:** If you have questions or critique regarding the *Open Intent* methodology, compliance, prompt design, or latent space aesthetics, please open a GitHub Discussion or submit an Issue.
- **Research & Dialogue:** If you are analyzing this project for academic, journalistic, or musicological purposes, please reach out via GitHub or the contact channels provided in the profile.


## 5. Humans in Latent Space: Call for Open Intent Repositories

*Likeness* is published under **[Humans in Latent Space](https://github.com/humans-in-latent-space)**, an organization and imprint dedicated to exploring human agency, craft, and accountability in generative latent spaces.

If you are developing independent projects that embrace the principles of **Open Intent**—rigorous creative provenance, full disclosure of machine orchestration, transparent prompt archives, and an uncompromising commitment to human artistic direction:

- **Get in touch:** Open an issue or discussion on GitHub to introduce your repository.
- **Ecosystem & Curation:** *Humans in Latent Space* is intended to actively track and showcase projects practicing transparent human-AI co-creation. The objective is to build a curated network of repositories that prove generative tools can serve human intentionality rather than obscure it.


## 6. Standards of Discourse and Moderation

While rigorous criticism, skepticism, and dissenting perspectives are genuinely welcomed, bad-faith remarks, content-free hostility, and derogatory attacks will be removed without comment or debate.

I hold deep respect for differing viewpoints and remain committed to fostering thoughtful, civil, and substantive dialogue. Those who choose contempt or hostility over good-faith engagement disqualify themselves from serious conversation and ultimately undermine their own position. The defining affliction of contemporary discourse is not individualism, but the reflexive drift toward outrage, panic-mongering, intellectual condescension, and shouting down opposing voices. If you participate here, do so in good faith.
