An experiment to explore the implications of AI-generated music.

The lyrics were initially created by Google Gemini (chat app), revised by a human, and finally submitted to Gemini or Lyria 3 (via the Gemini chat app) along with instructions for the musical arrangement, see `tracks`. For details regarding the concept and creative process see `session-notes`.

The license specified here applies only to the prompts and lyrics, not to the music files, as they do not meet the threshold of originality being purely AI-generated artifacts.
