# excellence

excellence.md
[Genre]: Indie-folk, acoustic alternative, chamber pop. Mood: Intimate, melancholic, analytical, tense.
[Vocal]: Female, extreme close-miking, breathy, articulate, dry, shifting to layered ghostly harmonies in the chorus. Instrumentation: Woody prepared piano, palm-muted acoustic guitar, mechanical clock-like percussive ticking, subtle sub-bass drone, minimal cello.
[​Progression]:
* Intro: Sparse and haptic. Isolated palm-muted acoustic guitar and prepared piano. Audible room tone and mechanical noise of the instruments.
* Verse 1: Close-mic lead vocal enters. An almost ticking rhythm starts, acting as the only percussion.
* Chorus: Stereo image widens. Vocal splits into ethereal, layered harmonies. Subtle, dark analog synth pads and a low drone swell in the background to create spatial depth.
* Verse 2:  The ticking percussion becomes slightly more complex, introducing subtle wooden clicks. A solo cello enters in the lower register, playing long, sustained notes against the staccato piano.
* Chorus 2: Maximum density. Fuller acoustic strumming, swelling strings, and prominent vocal layering.
* Bridge:  Sudden dynamic drop. Stripped back to just a single piano and one isolated, dry vocal. Tempo feels slightly rubato. Claustrophobic and highly intimate.
* Outro: The rhythm returns as a hypnotic, repetitive loop. Instruments slowly decay. The track ends on a single, unresolved muted piano note and a final mechanical tick.
